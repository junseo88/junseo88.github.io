Junseo Bang

Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate working at the intersection of optical imaging and generative AI. I develop diffusion/flow-based methods for solving inverse problems in imaging (advisor: Prof. Se Young Chun, Intelligent Computational Imaging Lab). Previously, I worked on advanced imaging systems with nanophotonic/metasurface technologies (advisors: Prof. Byoungho Lee, Prof. Yoonchan Jeong, Optical Engineering and Quantum Electronics Lab).

💡 Research interest: closing the gap between optical system design and learned signal reconstruction, developing next-generation intelligent imaging systems that integrate physics and machine intelligence.
