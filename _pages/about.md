Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate working at the intersection of optical imaging and generative AI.
I develop diffusion/flow-based methods for solving inverse problems in imaging (advisor: Prof.
[Se Young Chun](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=3jLuG64AAAAJ&hl=ko&oi=ao),
[Intelligent Computational Imaging Lab](https://icl.snu.ac.kr/)). Previously, I worked on
advanced imaging systems with nanophotonic/metasurface technologies (advisors: Prof.
[Byoungho Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VExwDP4AAAAJ&hl=ko&oi=ao), Prof.
[Yoonchan Jeong](http://oeqelab.snu.ac.kr/PROFJ),
[Optical Engineering and Quantum Electronics Lab](http://oeqelab.snu.ac.kr/)).

💡 My research interest is closing the gap between optical system design and learned signal
reconstruction, developing next-generation intelligent imaging systems that integrate physics
and machine intelligence.
